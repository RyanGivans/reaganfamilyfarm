A Farm Built on More Than Crops

What began in 1959 as a family farm has grown into a seasonal tradition for families across Northwest Arkansas. Through changing seasons and generations of care, Reagan Family Farm has remained a place where people can slow down, walk the rows, pick something fresh, and enjoy a little time outdoors.

Today, the farm continues to grow strawberries, blueberries, blackberries, raspberries, pumpkins, gourds, mums, cotton, and seasonal favorites while staying true to the same values that have always mattered most: family, care, and community.
